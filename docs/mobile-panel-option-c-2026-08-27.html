Ask Nanci on a phoneTwo things had to move: the menu and the panel

On a computer the menu sits down the left and a panel opens beside the conversation. A phone has room for neither. This is where each one went, and what the move costs.

The Sidebar

A phone cannot spare the width for a permanent rail, so the menu opens over the conversation from a single button and closes again.

On a phone
A drawer you summon

Closing it

The Panel

A phone has room for one panel and it has to sit on top. Put it away and it does not close: it shrinks to a handle above the message box, one swipe from returning.

Up to three, beside the chat
On a phone
One, over the chat
